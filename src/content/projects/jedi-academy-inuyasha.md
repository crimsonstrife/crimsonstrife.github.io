Inuyasha was my first character built for the Jedi Knight modding pipeline. I
made the character mesh and textures, rigged it for Jedi Academy, created the
cel-shading and outline materials, and packaged the result as a playable model.
The release includes the default red costume, a blue team variant, a demon-form
skin, and a separate Tetsusaiga weapon model with its own textures and game
configuration.

It was also an early exercise in adapting an anime design to a real-time game
skeleton while preserving the character's oversized hair, sleeves, silhouette,
and facial proportions. The JKHub listing has accumulated more than 6,000 views
and 200 downloads.

Released in 2008 as DarkLordXana/DLX, the preserved listing is attributed to my
current CrimsonStrife alias.
