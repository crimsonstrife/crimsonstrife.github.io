Same assignment as [Empty Skies](/projects/emptyskies/), same thousand-triangle
budget, and generally the easier of the two shapes. The Red Queen — Nero's sword
from *Devil May Cry* — is mostly a long straight blade, so the budget goes
almost entirely on the hilt.

720 triangles. Most of them ended up around the grip and the housing at the base
of the blade, which is the only part of the weapon with real depth to it; the
blade itself is a small number of long faces running its length. That split is
the whole lesson of the exercise — spend geometry where the shape turns, and
nowhere else.

Like the other one it stops at the mesh, untextured. The red and the trim in the
reference are doing a lot of work that geometry here isn't being asked to do,
which is the point: get the silhouette right cheaply, then let a texture carry
the rest.

The reference image is the game's art rather than mine.

Another early Stanly Community College piece, and like Empty Skies it reached
Sketchfab well after it was made.
