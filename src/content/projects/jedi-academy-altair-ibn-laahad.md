I built this fan-made Altaïr player model across the full asset pipeline: the
character mesh, layered costume and weapon props, UVs and textures, skeletal
rigging, and the final PK3 structure used by Jedi Academy. The material set
includes dedicated specular and environment maps for the cloth, leather,
metalwork, hood, blades, and bracers.

The model was completed in 2009 and publicly released in 2012. Its preserved
JKHub listing has accumulated more than 27,000 views, 1,000 downloads, and 14
reviews.

The original README credits my earlier DarkLordXana/DLX handle. The JKHub
listing was uploaded under CrimsonStrife, the alias I use today.
