This fan-made Ember McLain model began as a rendering experiment: I wanted a
ghost effect that felt luminous without falling back to Jedi Academy's familiar
hologram treatment. I modeled, textured, and rigged the character, then combined
cel-shaded materials, silhouette outlines, and pulsing light shaders to separate
the normal and energized looks shown in the original comparison image.

The mod includes alternate team skins and supporting textures for the eyes,
hair, glow, clothing, and material highlights. Its JKHub listing has since
passed 11,000 views and 200 downloads.

This was my second Jedi Academy character model, released in 2008 under the
DarkLordXana/DLX handle and later preserved on JKHub under CrimsonStrife.
