This requested fan model brought Guild Wars' Emperor Kisu into Jedi Academy.
I created the character model, rigged it for the game's skeleton, built the
texture and material set, wrote the shaders, and assembled the finished mod.
The ornate clothing used photo-sourced reference material, then separate
specular and environment maps to carry its silk, metal, and embroidered detail
in the older engine.

The package also includes bot and NPC definitions so the character works beyond
the multiplayer skin selector. The current JKHub listing has preserved the mod
with more than 8,000 views.

This 2008 release originally credited my DarkLordXana/DLX handle; its JKHub
